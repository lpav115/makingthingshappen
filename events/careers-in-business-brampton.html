April 27, 2025 · Our first night

Careers in Business

MTH brought high school students together with industry professionals at Brampton City Hall in our very first event. High school students learned about careers in business through professionals in private equity, consulting, startups, and strategy, and were taught to be intentional with their decisions, move with purpose, and take time to think about where they want their careers to go through an interactive panel and networking.

Event panelists

  • Harveen Sidhu

    Private Equity, Growth Equity & Venture Capital

  • Tony Liu

    Investment Banking & Strategic Finance

  • Mantej Gill

    Consulting & Accounting

  • Rashmi Jayaram

    Product & Marketing

Highlights of the night

What students walked away with

  1. Take time to think about what you really enjoy. Don't just blindly pursue the "proven path".
  2. Stay intellectually curious. Always speak with people about different careers and roles; the world changes fast.
  3. Your first few career decisions do not define your entire career. Few people know exactly what they want to do, and that's okay.
