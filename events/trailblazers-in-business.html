Past event · September 30, 2025 · HH 1101

Trailblazers in Business

An evening for SAF students to sit with their professors — and alumni in investment banking, private equity, and strategy — and hear how passion meets profession. Professors Steve Balaban, Garvin Blair, Konrad Pawlak, and Haaris Mian headlined the panel, then led intimate breakout sessions on their career paths.

Professors who trailblazed

  • Professor Steve Balaban

    Private Equity, Venture Capital & Wealth Mgmt.

  • Professor Garvin Blair

    Mergers and Acquisitions & Strategic Finance

  • Professor Konrad Pawlak

    Accounting and Finance Fundamentals

  • Professor Haaris Mian

    Data Analytics and Business Strategy
