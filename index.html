We're recent alumni helping students figure out what they actually want to do — in their career and in life.

We were just in your shoes. We know its impossible to make career decisions without feeling pressured. We host events to relieve that pressure.

Events that help you make the right next step in your career

We run events with alumni from various paths, including business, finance, technology, consulting, entrepreneurship and more, to help you decide on the right next step of your career using their unique experiences and advice.

01

Intentionality

Every night is designed so students leave clearer on their next move — not just inspired for an evening.

02

03

Breakouts and open networking make space for the hard questions speakers and peers are actually ready to answer.
