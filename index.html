What we do

Nights that help you decide

We run panel nights with alumni and professors who've walked messy paths — then break into smaller rooms so students can ask the questions that don't fit a stage mic. No recycled advice. Just honest conversation about career and life.

01

Intentionality

Every night is designed so students leave clearer on their next move — not just inspired for an evening.

02

Purpose

We push past job titles to help students connect what they love with how they want to work.

03

Curiosity

Breakouts and open networking make space for the hard questions speakers and peers are actually ready to answer.
