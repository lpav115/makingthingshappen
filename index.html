We're recent alumni helping students figure out what they actually want to do, so that they have fulfilling and successful careers.

We were just in your shoes. We know it's impossible to make career decisions without feeling pressured. We host events to relieve that pressure.

Events that help you make the right next step in your career.

We run events with alumni from various paths, including business, finance, technology, consulting, entrepreneurship and more, to help you decide on the right next step of your career using their unique experiences and advice.

01

Intentionality

We help students make their next steps with conviction, instead of doing for the sake of doing.

02

Purpose

We push past job titles to help students connect what they love with how they want to work.

03

Curiosity

We teach students how to dive deep into career paths that interest them, and encourage them to always explore what's out there.
